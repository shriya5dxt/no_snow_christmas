Weather

Phoenix will be sunny and mild. The canyon is 6,000 ft higher and about 25°F colder, which is proper winter. These are typical late-December numbers for the places on your route, not a forecast.

Reading the forecast
  • Winter Storm Warning for Flagstaff or the Grand Canyon on Dec 23–24: switch on "Snow forecast" and do the Sedona day trip instead.
  • Winter Weather Advisory: still go, but leave later, drive in daylight and add an hour.
  • Clear, or light flurries: go as planned. Fresh snow on the canyon is a bonus.
When to check
  • Dec 16: first look at the 7-day forecast from the National Weather Service in Flagstaff, which covers the Grand Canyon.
  • Dec 21 evening: final call on the canyon or the snow plan.
  • Each morning before driving north: AZ511 for closures, and the Grand Canyon NPS alerts.
What to pack
  • Layers: a base layer, a fleece, and an insulated or down jacket.
  • A hat, gloves, wool socks, and hand warmers for the rim sunrise.
  • Shoes with grip, plus microspikes for Ooh Aah Point.
  • Sunglasses, sunscreen and lip balm. The air is dry and the sun is strong at altitude.
  • A headlamp for sunrise and after-sunset walks.
On the road
  • I-17 climbs from 1,100 ft to 7,000 ft. It can be snowing in Flagstaff while it's 66°F in Phoenix.
  • Black ice forms on shaded curves until about 10:00. The 89A switchbacks through Oak Creek Canyon freeze first.
  • Keep the tank above half up north, and carry water, snacks and a blanket.
Daylight
  • Sunrise is around 07:30 and sunset 17:15–17:25, so you get about 10 hours of light.
  • Be off trails by sunset. The desert loses heat fast once the sun is down, even in Phoenix.
Phoenix days
  • Mornings are around 46°F (8°C) and afternoons in the mid-60s.
  • Bring a jacket for the Dec 22 sunset and the 06:00 Christmas-morning start.
  • Rain is rare in December. If it does rain, try the Musical Instrument Museum or the Heard Museum.

The options

Dec 22 is a half day, Dec 23–24 are both off, and Christmas morning has room for one more outing. Three ways to do the Grand Canyon, Flagstaff and Sedona on Dec 23–24. Sedona is only 2 hours away, so nobody sleeps there.
